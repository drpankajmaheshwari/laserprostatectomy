Privacy Policy

Dr Pankaj N Maheshwari · laserprostatectomy.com · Last updated: 31 October 2026

This website, laserprostatectomy.com, is the personal professional website of Dr Pankaj N Maheshwari, Director of Urology, Fortis Hospital Mulund, Mumbai 400080, India. This policy explains what personal information is handled when you use it or contact Dr Maheshwari.

Information this website collects

The website does not have accounts, forms or a booking system. It does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics tools, and it does not sell or rent any information. The web hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.

Information you choose to send

If you contact Dr Maheshwari through the contact form, by email or on WhatsApp, we receive the details you give, such as your name, phone number, email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to reply to you, to arrange care or follow-up, and to keep a record of the communication as professional practice requires.

Contact form

The “Ask a question / Contact me” form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number and your message, and for your consent. When you send it, these details are delivered to Dr Maheshwari’s office email address and are used only to reply to you. The form is handled by a third-party form-delivery service while it is switched on, so your entries pass through that provider’s servers, which may be outside India, on their way to us. If the form is not in use, your email program opens with the message instead, and it is sent through your own email provider. The form is not for emergencies or medical diagnosis, and you should not enter sensitive medical details in it.

WhatsApp

If you message or are messaged on WhatsApp, including WhatsApp Business, your phone number and messages are also processed by WhatsApp and Meta under their own terms and privacy policy. You can ask us to stop messaging you at any time by replying “STOP” or by writing to the email address below.

Medical information

Please do not send sensitive medical records or images through email or messaging unless Dr Maheshwari’s office has asked you to. Medical information is kept confidential and is shared only as the law and professional ethics allow.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing. We may disclose information where the law requires it, or where it is needed for your care, with your knowledge.

Retention and your rights

We keep communications only as long as needed for the purposes above and as the law requires. You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to withdraw consent for communication, by writing to us. We will respond as required under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Medical information on this site

Information on this website is for general patient education. It is not a substitute for a medical consultation, and it does not promise any particular result.
